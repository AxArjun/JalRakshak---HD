# JalRakshak-HD: Key Scientific & Engineering Differentiators

JalRakshak-HD is engineered with strict scientific defensibility, transparent provenance, and operational realism.

### 1. Dual-Solver Multi-Scale Hydrodynamic Framework
Unlike standard GIS flood viewers that rely on static planar slope calculations or simple 1D approximations, JalRakshak-HD integrates:
- **3D DualSPHysics (SPH):** Resolves Navier-Stokes free-surface particle mechanics ($10,982$ particles) for extreme near-field structure splash, jetting, and wall impacts ($0$–$600\text{ s}$).
- **2D D-Flow Flexible Mesh (Delft3D FM):** Resolves Shallow Water Equations across an $818.37\text{ km}^2$ domain over a full $30\text{-hour}$ propagation cycle ($181$ timesteps) with mass conservation verified ($0.0052\%$ residual).

### 2. Multi-Source Population Exposure Transparency
Rather than blending or averaging contradictory census datasets, JalRakshak-HD provides dual-perspective exposure screening:
- **WorldPop ($42,428$ exposed):** Ambient residential night-time population baseline.
- **GHSL ($84,501$ exposed):** Built-up structural footprint and daytime economic activity density.
- This empowers disaster managers to plan for both minimum residential and maximum daytime evacuation loads.

### 3. All-Weather Satellite Earth Observation Benchmarking
Integrates Copernicus Sentinel-1 Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) to validate water surface boundaries independent of cloud cover, rain, or night conditions, establishing historical flood baselines.

### 4. Zero-Dependency Offline GIS Resilience
Designed for field disaster deployment:
- If satellite internet fails, the command centre automatically falls back to local NASA SRTM 30m hillshade rasters and vector caches.
- Complete playback, point inspection, and consequence metrics remain $100\%$ functional offline.

### 5. Configuration-Driven Portability (Hirakud Generalization)
All domain bounds, dam engineering parameters, and hydrologic equations are parameterized via declarative YAML configuration files. Demonstrated by onboarding Hirakud Dam without altering backend code.

### 6. Strict Scientific Honesty & Validation Gated Architecture
No simulated data is ever disguised as real, and no unrun simulations are fabricated. Every number traces directly to authoritative source manifests.
